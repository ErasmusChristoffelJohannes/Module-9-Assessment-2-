About Claire

Hungry, curious, and always carrying a camera.

I'm Claire, and FoodieFanatix is where I share every meal worth crossing a border for. I visit anonymously, pay my own way, and photograph everything before the first bite. If a place is great, I'll tell you what to order. If it isn't, I'll tell you that too.

How the Fanatix score works

  • 9.0+Book the flight. Worth planning a trip around.
  • 8.0–8.9Worth a detour. Cross town for it.
  • 7.0–7.9Good if you're nearby. Solid, but not a pilgrimage.
  • < 7.0Skip it. Your appetite deserves better.
